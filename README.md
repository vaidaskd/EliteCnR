Custom Cops and Robbers Server
FiveM: Legacy

Elite Cops & Robbers is a FiveM server where players can choose between Law Enforcement and Robbers. Cops earn credits by arresting wanted criminals, while robbers earn money and credits through robberies and criminal activities.

The server focuses on simple, competitive cops-vs-criminals gameplay with a persistent credit system, police equipment, vehicles, robberies, jail time, and a wanted system.

Basic Rules
Respect other players and do not intentionally disrupt their gameplay.
Do not exploit bugs or abuse server mechanics.
Do not use cheats, hacks, or unauthorized software.
Do not intentionally kill innocent players as a cop.
Cops can only cuff and arrest wanted robbers.
Innocent robbers become wanted after committing crimes.
Killing players or NPCs while wanted increases jail time.
Stealing police vehicles can result in additional jail time and wanted status.
Follow staff instructions and server decisions.
Keep gameplay fair and enjoyable for everyone.
